Selected earlier work

The programs behind the numbers.

Complex programs across digital learning, education, economic development, and humanitarian response. A few that still shape how the practice works.

Community learning

Community-Based Learning & Action Model

Born from flipping a culture of waiting for handouts into one of local action. The model mobilized untapped human and social capital and put communities in the driver's seat, spreading from Palestine to Jordan, Lebanon, Yemen, and Morocco. Twenty years on it still runs, self reliant, with full community ownership.

20 years · 100% community owned

Digital learning

Netketabi

A national digital learning program built from nothing: original content, the systems to deliver it, and the digital skills young people need to become builders and problem solvers themselves. The point was never the technology. It was opening a pathway for a generation growing up in a fragile context.

$10M contract · $500K raised

Education systems

Model School Networks

A replicable model linking model schools to community learning and development centers, so that what a school learned, its community kept. Digital skills are not built only in a classroom. They are built in the community too.

Design & technical lead

Refugee education

Remedial Education for Refugee Children

Remedial learning for refugee children in host communities: meeting displaced children where their schooling was interrupted and building the footing they need to catch up and rejoin the classroom.
