Community plan to action

Many community plans are finished and then left on a shelf. This system is meant to keep a plan in use: each priority is tied to the department responsible, the actions agreed, a way to tell whether it is working, and regular reporting back to leadership and community.

Priorities
Each priority in the plan has an owner.

The department responsible, the actions agreed and the dates are in one place, in the community's own words.

Progress
A simple way to tell whether it is working.

A small number of measures for each priority, chosen with the people who do the work, in place of a long list nobody reads.

Reporting
Regular reporting back to leadership and community.

The plan is looked at again on a set rhythm, so it changes as the community's situation changes.

Where it stands
Being designed now, shown with a made up example.

It holds no Nation's information. A Nation's own plan would go into it only with that Nation's decision. Community planning with First Nations
