Seaweed: economics, business and markets

Know the numbers before you commit the money.

People working with seaweed often have to decide with very little published information. What a farmer is paid, what processing costs and who is buying can be hard to find, and sometimes no figure has been published at all.

Visit Seaweed Economy
How the work is done

Gathered, sourced and dated.

Gathered
What has been published is brought into one place.

Prices, costs and buyer information for Canada and the United States come from public reports, studies and sector organisations. The people and groups who published them are named.

Sourced and dated
Each figure shows where it came from and when.

You can check any number against its source and judge for yourself how far it applies to your place and your product.

Gaps stated
Where no reliable figure has been published, that is said plainly.

No number is invented to fill a gap. Knowing what is not known is part of making a sound decision.
